How it works

Four steps from "I need to break something" to walking out lighter.

1

Pick a package

Choose a rage room or axe throwing package below. Each one sets how much you'll smash.

2

Choose your group size, then book

Select how many people are coming. You'll land on our scheduling tool to pick a day, time, and pay.

3

Sign the waiver

Hit the waiver link after booking, or fill it out ahead of time at our online waiver.

4

Arrive 10 minutes early

Wear closed-toed shoes and bring your completed waiver. We'll hand you safety gear and weapons before you go in — the room's already loaded with breakables.
